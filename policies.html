The App displays ads served by Google AdMob. Ads may be personalised based on your interests. To do this, advertising partners may use your device's Advertising ID, a resettable identifier provided by Android.

You can limit personalised advertising at any time:

  • On most Android devices, open Settings → Google → Ads (or Settings → Privacy → Ads) and choose to reset or delete your Advertising ID, or opt out of ads personalisation.

If you opt out, you will still see ads, but they may be less relevant to you.
