FAQ

Questions, answered

What does Anime AI do?

Anime AI is an AI anime filter for Android. It turns your selfies into anime photos and helps you create a unique anime avatar based on your pictures.

How do I turn a photo into anime?

Select a picture, choose a style and let the app work. Your anime version is ready in seconds. You can also describe details you would like to add to your anime photo.

Does it only work on selfies?

No. Whether it's your friend, your pet or an object, Anime AI can turn the photo into an anime-styled picture.

Can I save and share my results?

Yes. Save your anime photo to your gallery or share it with your friends on social media.
